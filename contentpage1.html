Believe it or not the first
flying car was built and
flown in 1946!!

Many people believe flying cars are a dream of the distant future - but the truth is that they’ve already taken flight in the 1940s. Post war, during the height of optimism, engineers believed the future soared across the skies.
Pioneers such as Glenn Curtiss in 1917 and Constantinos Vlachos in 1935 attempted to fly us through the skies of the future but their attempts were futile due to the primitive technology available during the early 20th century (Popular Science, 1917; Wikipedia, 2025).
Why aren't we commuting though the skies today? The world wasn't ready for such innovation, the publics trust was thin and the growing infrastructure was built for roads not runways.
